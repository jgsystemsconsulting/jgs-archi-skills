# View Rationale: Motivation Overview

## Purpose
Show why Meridian Freight must change: which stakeholders feel which drivers, what evidence exists, and which goals, outcomes, and requirements the uplift must serve.

## Stakeholders and Concerns
COO and CFO care about margin and cost; Head of Customer Service about quote speed and consistency; IT Manager about integration debt and data growth; Customers about visibility.

## Viewpoint
Motivation viewpoint (standard): stakeholders, drivers, assessments, goals, outcomes, requirements only.

## Questions Answered
Why is change needed, what evidence supports it, and which four goals define success?

## Assumptions
The frozen scenario's driver and goal lists are complete for this scope; percentages are accepted as given.

## Decisions
Assessments influence drivers with negative strength to mark aggravating evidence; goals influence outcomes (bridge-validated: realization is not legal between goal and outcome); requirements realize goals they materially advance.

## Exclusions
Principles, value, meaning, and constraints are out of the frozen scenario scope.

## Open Questions
None within scenario scope; weight of each influence (+ versus ++) was not user-calibrated.
