§02 · Stand up

Stand up the practice

Install JGS Archi Bridge and this pack on the hosts they name. Host paths: docs/other-agents.md.

First /archi-orchestrator call on a non-secret intent, or on a throwaway model. Stop at the View Plan gate. They approve or reject. The firm does not write their architecture unless they also buy a delivery package. Sample asks: How you ask.

Done when: MCP shows Stop MCP Server, skills are visible after an agent restart, they have issued one orchestrator line, and they have approved or rejected the View Plan.

Out of scope here: estate redraw, prompt-card authoring, a production model.

§03 · Delivery

Delivery work packages

One concern, one View Plan, their model on their disk. Extra packages cover extra journeys or an adjacent pair.

PackageWhat lands in ArchiDone when
Motivation packDrivers, goals, outcomes. No applications on the canvasBoard can read why the change is on the list
Single capability cutOne value stream: capabilities plus the processes and applications that already cover themPath from a capability through a process to an application
Business operations viewActors, roles, processes, services for one journeyJourney is in Archi

Technology / landing zoneAs-is hosts versus target. Business not redesignedPlateaus exist; no invented business process
Migration roadmapWork packages, gaps, plateaus on a model that already has current and targetRoadmap is in the model
Layout and QA onlyRelayout plus a compliance report. No new elementsView readable; findings listed

Client supplies a running Archi plus Bridge, or buys standup first. Extra journeys, extra stakeholders, or an unbounded estate are extra packages. Adjacent pair (current versus target plus roadmap) is still a short list of packages, not a programme contract. User governs architectural decisions. Skills stay consume-only toward the Bridge.

§04 · Enablement

Enablement

View Plan clinic

Sponsors and leads practise approve or reject, including what a specialist may write.

They can reject a plan that exceeds the named altitudes.

Method studio

Architects drive the orchestrator on a non-secret scenario. House style, layer cuts, local defect versus upstream defect.

Two or three of them can complete a run to View Plan without the firm at the keyboard.

Named review

Monthly read of their View Plans or a model excerpt. Challenge cut, traces, and scope.

Written comments on the plans they send, on an agreed turnaround.

Public GitHub Issues stay for pack defects every user would hit. Named review is their architecture.
